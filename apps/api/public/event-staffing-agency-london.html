Events Covered

Event staffing changes by format. A private dinner needs a different team from a corporate launch, and a production kitchen needs different support from a drinks reception. VERGO’s role is to clarify the brief and avoid vague “send staff” bookings that leave too much to chance.

For clients who already know exactly what they need, the process can be direct. For less defined briefs, the first step is understanding the event shape and recommending the right team size.

Common event types

  • Corporate launches, conferences, and hospitality suites
  • Private dining, weddings, and celebrations
  • Film, TV, and production catering
  • Festivals, concerts, and arena hospitality
  • Brand activations, pop-ups, and retail events

London Coverage

VERGO supports event staffing enquiries across London, including the City, Mayfair, Soho, Shoreditch, Kensington, Chelsea, Canary Wharf, Westminster, Camden, Hackney, Greenwich, Battersea, Richmond, and surrounding areas. For outer London or nearby counties, share the postcode and timings so travel and availability can be checked clearly.

How the Booking Process Works

  • Send the event date, location, timings, guest count, and role requirements.
  • VERGO recommends a staffing route and confirms commercial terms in writing.
  • Staff receive the relevant brief before the shift, including dress code and venue notes.
  • After the event, the booking is reconciled and invoiced.
