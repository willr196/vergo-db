Answers

The usual questions.

How many staff do we need for a Christmas party?

Tell us the guest number, the service style and the timings and we’ll recommend a team for your event rather than applying a ratio to it. A seated dinner needs a different team from a drinks reception with canapés.

Our party runs past midnight. How does that work?

Hours past the confirmed finish are billed in 30-minute blocks, and work after midnight is charged at +25%. Tell us the real finish time when you enquire and it’s in the quote rather than on the invoice.

How late can we book Christmas staff?

Ask whatever the date. Enquire between 8am and 10pm and you get confirmed names back the same day, so you’ll know quickly either way. The more notice we have, the more choice you get.

What happens if someone doesn’t turn up?

We have a replacement on site within the hour. If we can’t, that shift isn’t charged.

Can we cancel a Christmas booking?

More than 48 hours before the booking starts, there’s no charge. Inside 48 hours it’s 10% of the confirmed booking charge, and inside 24 hours it’s 25%. The full terms are on the terms page.

Booking staff for a Christmas party?

Send the date and the guest number and we’ll come back with the team and the cost.
