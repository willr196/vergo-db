Our story

About us

A small, founder-led event and hospitality staffing company in London. We send people we’ve met, interviewed and briefed, and we answer for them on the night.

Where it started

I’m Will Robb. I grew up around event staffing: my family ran an event-staffing business, so I saw early what a good team does for a night, and what a poor one costs the people who booked it.

Since then I’ve spent more than eight years working in London hospitality, film and television production, and live music. Different rooms, the same lesson every time. An event goes well when the people working it know what’s expected of them before they walk in.

Too often that isn’t how staffing works. An agency sends whoever is free, nobody has read the brief, and the client spends the first hour explaining the job. I started VERGO to do it the other way round.

What that means in practice

  • I interview every worker on the roster myself. Nobody is sent to your event off a CV alone
  • Every booking is briefed: the venue, the service style and what the night involves
  • Our employed team get holiday pay, pension and payslips, not a self-employed invoice
  • Right to work checked and documented for everyone we send
  • Employers’ and public liability insured, with certificates on request

It’s also why we can put two guarantees in writing: confirmed names the same day, and a replacement on site within the hour if anyone fails to arrive, or that shift isn’t charged. The details are on our client page.
