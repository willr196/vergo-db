What we can take on

The food.

Canapés and nibbles for a drinks reception, breakfasts for an early start, sit-down dinners, and anything in between. Tell us the guests and the style and we will put a menu together.

The whole bar.

We can bring the bar with us: the drinks, the set-up and the bartenders to run it. Or just the bartenders, if the venue already has the rest.

The people.

Waiting staff, bar staff, kitchen porters, runners and hosts, plus anything more niche, like photography or manual labour for the set-up and clear-down.

01
