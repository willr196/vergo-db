Answers

The usual questions.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. The last week of October is our busiest of the year, and performers and makeup artists commit sooner than hospitality staff. Ask anyway — we will tell you what is still possible.

Do you supply the costumes?

Yours, styled by us, or sourced and hired for the booking. We do not hold a costume store, so raise the look early.

How scary is “scary”?

Your call, agreed before the event. Scare level, contact, age range and venue limits are all set in the brief.

Is every VERGO worker an actor?

No. Themed hospitality staff hold a character but the job comes first. Performers and scare actors are booked separately and cast for the role.

Can we book one or two people?

Yes. Two themed hosts is a normal booking. Standard staffing minimums still apply to hospitality shifts — see the hire page.

How does guest makeup work?

Artists set up inside the event and work through guests during the evening. Give us guest count and running time and we will say how many artists it needs.

What do you need to quote?

Date, rough hours, venue or area, approximate guest count, and the feeling you are after.

Agreed up front

Scare level, guest interaction, age range, venue restrictions and performance boundaries.
