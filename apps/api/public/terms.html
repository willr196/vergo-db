Terms of business

First draft: not yet reviewed by a solicitor. This page sets out our intended terms based on our current understanding of the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003. Anything marked [TO CONFIRM] is a placeholder and must be filled in or checked with a solicitor before this page is treated as binding or published as final.

These terms apply whenever VERGO LTD ("VERGO", "we", "us") supplies or introduces staff to a client ("you", "the hirer"). VERGO LTD is an employment business under the Employment Agencies Act 1973 and the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003. See our company and regulatory details.

1. Enquiries and bookings

The running total shown on our quote form is an estimate, not a binding quote. A booking is only confirmed once we send you written confirmation (email or WhatsApp) naming the staff, roles, date and times.

Enquiries submitted during our confirmation hours (8am–10pm) are confirmed quickly. See our guarantees for the full detail.

2. Rates and Charges

  • £18 per hour, per person, for waiting staff, bar staff, kitchen porters, runners and hosts. Rates are confirmed on your quote
  • VERGO LTD is not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged
  • 4-hour minimum charge per person, per booking
  • Overruns beyond the confirmed end time are billed in 30-minute blocks
  • Hours worked after midnight are charged at +25%
  • Supervisors, chefs, event managers, performers, makeup artists and themed roles are quoted separately and agreed in writing before booking
  • No booking fees, uniform charges or other extras beyond what is set out here

3. Payment

VERGO invoices after the booking. Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date.

4. Changes and Cancellation

  • More than 48 hours before the booking starts: no charge
  • Inside 48 hours: 10% of the confirmed booking value
  • Inside 24 hours: 25% of the confirmed booking value

These bands apply to the booking as a whole. If you need to reduce staff numbers rather than cancel entirely, tell VERGO as early as possible and a fair adjustment will be agreed.

5. Our guarantees

Same-day names. Enquire between 8am and 10pm and confirmed names come back that day.

No-show. If a member of staff we've supplied fails to arrive, we'll have a replacement on site within 60 minutes. If we can't, that shift isn't charged.

6. Right to work, employment status and insurance

  • Every worker VERGO supplies is employed by VERGO LTD on a PAYE basis, with holiday pay, payslips and pension where applicable
  • Right to work is checked and documented for every worker before their first assignment with you
  • We hold employers' liability and public liability insurance. Certificates are available on request, policy details: [TO CONFIRM]

7. Décor and third-party suppliers

VERGO supplies people, the brief and, where booked, a lead on the floor. Décor, props, production and anything else from a third party is arranged on request through a supplier, quoted separately, and supplied on that supplier's terms.

8. Introducing or employing a worker directly (temp-to-perm)

If you want to take on a worker we've introduced or supplied to you directly (whether you employ them yourself or they're supplied to you by another agency after an introduction from us), tell us first. This section explains what happens next, in line with the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003 (regulation 10).

You will always have a choice. Instead of paying an introduction/transfer fee, you can instead choose to keep hiring that worker through VERGO for a further extended-hire period of [TO CONFIRM] weeks, on the same rates as before, at the end of which they transfer to you free of charge. That choice is yours to make, not ours.

If you'd rather pay a fee instead of taking the extended-hire option, the fee is [TO CONFIRM].

This only applies if direct employment begins within the "relevant period" defined by the regulations: broadly, within 8 weeks of the worker's last assignment with you through VERGO, or 14 weeks from their first assignment with you, whichever ends later. Outside that period, no fee applies at all.

No fee is ever charged to the worker in connection with this: not for taking up work with you, and not for taking up work found through any other agency. That's a legal requirement (regulation 5 of the same regulations), not just our policy.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything else that can't be limited by law. Beyond that, our liability to you in connection with a booking is limited to [TO CONFIRM].

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The current version always applies to new bookings from the date it's published here.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.
