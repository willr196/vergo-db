What Makes Festival Staffing Different

Festival and large-format events need staff who can handle pace, changing conditions, and clear reporting lines. The brief should explain zones, access, wristbands, breaks, staff meals, call times, uniform, and who is managing the team on site.

For high-volume bars or hospitality areas, team size should be planned around peak arrival times rather than total attendance alone.

Details to send

  • Site location, entrance, and accreditation process
  • Zones or areas where staff will work
  • Shift times, break plan, and expected finish
  • Role list and expected headcount per role
  • Uniform, PPE, and weather considerations

Operational Details Matter

Festival staffing is rarely just about headcount. Staff need to know where to arrive, how accreditation works, what area they are assigned to, who signs them in, where breaks happen, and what to do if the site layout changes. Clear logistics make a meaningful difference on outdoor and high-volume events.

For bar-heavy events, include expected peak times, till or token systems, stock handling, and whether barbacks are already planned. For hospitality areas, include guest profile and service expectations.

FAQs

Can VERGO staff multiple zones?

Yes, where the brief is clear. Share the zone list, role allocation, and who manages each area.

Can you help with high-volume bar staffing?

Yes. Send the number of bars, expected peaks, drink format, and whether barback support is required.

Can staff work outdoors?

Yes, subject to role and conditions. Include weather considerations, uniform requirements, and welfare arrangements.
