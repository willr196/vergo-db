FAQ

Pricing questions.

What does "agreed wage" mean?

The hourly rate paid to the staff member. It depends on the role, experience, and your event requirements. We confirm this with you before any commitment - you always see the full breakdown.

How quickly do I get a quote?

Within 24 hours of submitting your enquiry.

What does the Standard tier cover?

Standard connects you with vetted self-employed workers from the VERGO roster. Pricing is the agreed wage plus the VERGO fee and VAT.

When is Gold the right fit?

Gold is for senior and high-trust placements, including guest-facing roles, productions, and major events. It uses fixed public rates.

Can I combine Standard and Gold on one event?

Yes. One event can use Gold for senior roles and Standard for the wider team.

Is VAT included in the prices shown?

No. VAT is added on top of the Standard total and on top of the Gold hourly rates shown on this page.

Do you offer ongoing commercial rates?

Yes. If you need regular staffing support, we can quote custom commercial terms and dedicated account management.
