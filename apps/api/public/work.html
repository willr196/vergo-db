What VERGO asks

Turn up.

On time, every time. If you cannot make a shift, say so as early as you can and it gets covered. Not turning up without a word is the one thing VERGO does not work with.

Look the part.

Each event has its own dress code. VERGO confirms it before the shift and provides kit where it can.

Be straight about your experience.

Experience is preferred, but for the right role VERGO will train you.

Straight answers

Am I employed?

Yes. You are on the VERGO LTD payroll on PAYE. Tax and National Insurance are deducted, you get a payslip, and there is nothing to sort out yourself at the end of the year.

Do I have to take shifts?

No. Shifts are offered, not compulsory. Turning one down does not cost you the next.

How much work is there?

It depends on your availability and how flexible you are. People free at weekends and in December get offered more.

Do I need experience?

For bar and silver service, yes. For runners and kitchen porters, no. Be reliable and willing.

How old do I need to be?

18 or over.
