Questions

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can: the last week of October books up first. Ask anyway and we'll tell you what's still possible.

Do you supply the costumes?

Yours styled by us, or sourced and hired for the booking. We don't hold a costume store, so raise the look early.

How scary is “scary”?

Your call, agreed before the event. Scare level, contact, age range and venue limits all go in the brief.

Can we book one or two people?

Yes, two themed hosts is a normal booking. The {{MIN_HOURS}}-hour minimum still applies.
