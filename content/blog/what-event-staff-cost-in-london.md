## How does the four hour minimum work?

Every person on a booking is charged for at least four hours. A two hour drinks reception with five waiting staff is billed as five people for four hours each: 5 x 4 x 18 = 360 pounds.

In practice the minimum rarely changes the bill. Staff arrive before guests to set up and a room still needs clearing after the last guest leaves, so most shifts run past four hours on their own. Where it does apply is the short, tight booking: a one hour canapé round before a dinner, or a host on the door for the first ninety minutes.

If your event is short, say so in the brief. VERGO will tell you straight away whether the minimum applies and what it means for the total.

## What happens when the night runs late?

Two separate things can happen, and the rate card treats them separately.

The first is an overrun. If the event goes past the finish time on the confirmed booking, the extra time is billed in 30 minute blocks. Twenty minutes over is half an hour, not a full hour. Ninety minutes over is three blocks.

The second is midnight. Any hour worked after midnight is charged at plus 25 percent, whether it was planned or not. On a core role that is 22.50 pounds an hour instead of 18.

The midnight uplift is on the rate card, and in every quote, because late finishes are common. Weddings, Christmas parties and private parties at home all run past twelve more often than not. The fix is simple: give the real finish time when you enquire, and the uplift is in the quote rather than a surprise on the invoice.

## What does a real booking cost?

A private party at home. Six staff: four waiting, two on the bar. Staff on from 7pm, finishing at 12:30am.

- Five hours before midnight: 6 x 5 x 18 = 540 pounds
- Thirty minutes after midnight: 6 x 0.5 x 22.50 = 67.50 pounds

## What is quoted separately, and why?

Some roles vary too much from one brief to the next to carry a single number. Those are quoted per booking and confirmed in writing before you book:

- Supervisors and event managers
- Chefs
- Performers and character actors
- Makeup artists
- Themed roles for seasonal events

A supervisor for a 40 person dinner is a different job from an event manager running a 300 person launch. A makeup artist working on six performers is a different job from one running a guest station all night. One flat figure would be wrong for most of them, so VERGO prices them on the brief instead.

The core rate does not change when these roles are added. If a Christmas party books ten waiting staff and a supervisor, the ten waiting staff are still 18 pounds an hour each.

## Why does "no VAT" matter when comparing quotes?

Because a headline rate and a final rate are not the same thing.

Many staffing agencies quote an hourly rate with VAT to be added. At 20 percent, a quoted 17 pounds becomes 20.40 pounds an hour. A quoted 18 pounds becomes 21.60 pounds. The number on the front of the quote looks lower than VERGO's, and the number on the invoice is higher.

Whether that matters depends on who is paying. A VAT registered business can usually reclaim the VAT, so for them the headline rate is close to the real cost. A private host cannot. Neither can many charities, schools or small businesses under the threshold. For them the VAT is simply part of the price.

The only fair comparison is the final figure. Put both quotes side by side at the amount you will actually pay, for the same number of people and the same hours, including any minimums and late finish charges. Then compare.

## What should you ask any agency before you book?

Five questions make any two quotes comparable:

1. Is VAT included in the rate you quoted, or added on top?
2. What is the minimum booking per person?
3. How are overruns billed: by the hour, or in smaller blocks?
4. What changes after midnight?
5. Are the staff employed on PAYE, and what happens if someone does not turn up?

The last question is not about price, but it decides what you get for the price. VERGO employs every person it sends on PAYE, and if someone fails to arrive, a replacement is on site within the hour. If VERGO cannot do that, the shift is not charged.
