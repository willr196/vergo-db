What they do on the night

01

The reception.

Trays out on time, drinks topped up, and canapes described properly to anyone who asks what is on them.

02

The dinner.

Service to your style, whether that is plated, silver or sharing. Courses land together rather than in ones and twos.

03

The clear down.

Tables cleared through the evening, then the room put back the way the venue expects to find it.

Most bookings are one of those three, or all of them in a single evening. Tell us which and the brief is written around it.

Who books waiting staff from us

  • Caterers who have the food covered and need the floor covered
  • Venues running more in one week than their own team can staff
  • Production companies and private clients putting on a one-off
  • Agencies needing overflow cover at short notice

We supply people only. Glassware, crockery, linen and stock stay with you or your caterer, and the team is briefed on what they will be working with.

What to tell us when you enquire

  • Date, start time and the real finish time, not the one on the invitation
  • Venue and address, including whether it is a dry-hire space
  • Guest numbers and the service style: reception, seated, or both
  • What is being served, and who is cooking it
  • Anything unusual about the room, the access or the timings

That is enough for us to recommend a team size and send the quote back the same day.

Questions we get asked

How many waiting staff do we need?

Tell us the guest number, the service style and the timings and we will recommend a team for your event rather than applying a ratio to it. A standing reception with canapes and a seated three-course dinner need different teams for the same headcount.

Do your waiting staff know the running order before they arrive?

Yes. Everyone we send has been met and interviewed, and is briefed on the venue, the service style and what the evening involves before they arrive. Send us the running order when you have it and it goes into that brief.

What is the minimum booking for waiting staff?

Four hours per person. Overruns are billed in 30-minute blocks, and hours worked after midnight are charged at plus 25%. There are no booking fees, uniform charges or surcharges on top.

Do you supply glassware, crockery or linen?

No. We supply people only. Stock, equipment and hire items come from you or your caterer, and we brief the team on what they will be working with.

Need waiting staff for a date in the diary?

Send us the date, the venue and the roles and you get confirmed names back the same day, between 8am and 10pm.
