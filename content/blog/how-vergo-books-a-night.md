## In short

- A VERGO booking starts with the date, the venue, the headcount and what the room needs to feel like.
- Enquire between 8am and 10pm and confirmed names come back the same day, not a headcount.
- Everyone VERGO sends has been interviewed first and is briefed on the venue and the night before arriving.

- Every person is employed by VERGO LTD on PAYE, and the invoice is payable within 14 days.

## What does VERGO need to start a booking?

Four things: the date, the venue, the headcount, and what the room needs to feel like.

The first three are standard. The fourth is the one that changes who gets sent. A seated dinner for twelve in a private house needs different people from a 300 guest launch in a warehouse. A wedding with a tight running order needs different people from a relaxed garden party. The brief is how VERGO tells the difference.

Useful extras, if you have them:

- Start and finish times, including the real finish
- The service style: drinks and canapés, buffet, seated dinner, bar only
- Guest numbers, even roughly
- The dress code
- Anything unusual: a dry hire venue, a tight kitchen, stairs, a late licence

None of it has to be final. Half a brief is enough to start, and the gaps get filled in the reply.

## How fast does VERGO confirm staff?

The same day. Enquire between 8am and 10pm and confirmed names come back that day.

Names, not a headcount. A confirmation that says "four waiting staff" tells you nothing about who is walking through the door. VERGO sends the people, by name, and who does what: who is on the bar, who is running, who leads the floor if the booking has a lead.

Every one of those people has been met and interviewed by VERGO before being offered a shift. Nobody is picked from an app because they happened to be free.

## What happens before the night?

Everyone on the booking gets the brief. The venue and how to get in. The arrival time. The dress code. The service style. Who to report to on arrival, and who makes decisions on the night.

The point is that nobody arrives asking what the job is. By the time they walk in, they know the room, the running order and what good looks like for this event. You should not have to spend the first half hour of your own party explaining it.

You also get a number. If anything changes, you call VERGO, not a switchboard, and it gets handled.

## What if someone does not turn up?

VERGO puts a replacement on site within the hour. If it cannot, that shift is not charged.

That rule is written into the terms, not left as a goodwill gesture. It exists because a no-show is the one failure a client cannot fix on the night, and the person paying should not carry the cost of it.

It is also the reason the rest of the process works the way it does. Interviewing everyone first, briefing everyone before the night and employing everyone properly all make a no-show less likely in the first place.

## Who employs the staff?

VERGO does. Every person VERGO sends is employed by VERGO LTD on PAYE, with payslips, holiday pay, and pension where applicable. Right to work is checked before anyone's first shift.

That matters to the client as much as to the worker. Payroll, holiday pay and employment status sit with VERGO, not with you. There is no question later about whether someone was really self employed, because nobody is.

VERGO also holds employers' liability and public liability insurance. Certificates are available on request, and plenty of venues ask for them before a booking. Say so in the brief and they come with the confirmation.

## How does the invoice work?

One invoice, after the booking, payable within 14 days.

The rate for waiting staff, bar staff, kitchen porters, runners and hosts is 18 pounds an hour per person. VERGO LTD is not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add. There is a four hour minimum per person, overruns are billed in 30 minute blocks, and hours after midnight carry a 25 percent uplift. All of that is on the quote before you book, so nothing on the invoice should be new. The full rate card is on the [Staff page](/hire).

Supervisors, chefs, event managers, performers, makeup and themed roles are quoted separately and confirmed in writing before the booking.

## What if the plans change?

They often do, and the terms are built for it.

- More than 48 hours before the booking starts: cancel for free.
- Inside 48 hours: 10 percent of the booked value.
- Inside 24 hours: 25 percent of the booked value.

If you only need fewer people rather than none, say so as early as you can and VERGO will agree a fair adjustment rather than treating it as a cancellation.

## What if the brief is bigger than staff?

Some nights need more than people. Christmas parties, Halloween events, themed nights and brand activations run on the same process, from the same roster, with two additions: VERGO proposes the tone as well as the team, and a VERGO lead runs the floor when the booking needs one.

Décor and production can be arranged through trusted suppliers on request, quoted separately. VERGO's own part is the people, the brief and the lead on the floor.

## FAQ

### What happens if a VERGO staff member does not show up?

VERGO puts a replacement on site within the hour. If it cannot, that shift is not charged. The rule is written into the terms of business.

### Are VERGO staff employed or self employed?

Employed. Every person VERGO sends is on the VERGO LTD payroll on PAYE, with payslips and holiday pay. Right to work is checked before the first shift.

### When do I pay for a VERGO booking?

VERGO invoices after the booking. The invoice is payable within 14 days. There is no VAT to add, because VERGO LTD is not VAT registered.
