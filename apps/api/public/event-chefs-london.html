Details That Help Us Match Chefs Properly

A chef brief works best when it explains the menu, prep state, kitchen setup, equipment, service times, and who is leading the kitchen on site. If the event is still being planned, send the current version and flag anything that may change.

For production or temporary kitchens, access, load-in, refrigeration, handover notes, and close-down expectations are especially important.

Include where possible

  • Menu, prep level, and expected covers
  • Kitchen equipment and venue limitations
  • Service time, arrival time, and finish time
  • Chef level needed: CDP, sous, head chef, or support
  • On-site lead, dress code, and access instructions

FAQs

How quickly can you confirm event chefs?

Most enquiries are confirmed same-day where availability allows. For larger teams or specialist requirements, we’ll confirm as soon as the details are clear.

Do chefs bring their own knives and kit?

Yes, chefs attend event-ready with standard personal kit. If the venue has specific requirements, we include that in the pre-shift plan.

Can you supply a full kitchen team?

Yes. We can supply chefs plus support roles depending on your menu and service style. Tell us the format and headcount.
