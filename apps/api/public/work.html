Questions

Am I employed or self-employed?

Employed. You're on our payroll, we deduct your tax and National Insurance, and you get a payslip.

Do I have to take shifts?

No. You'll get offered work, and you take what suits you.

Do I need experience?

For bar and silver service, yes. For runners and kitchen porters, no: just be reliable.
