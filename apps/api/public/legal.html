2. Regulatory status

Vergo Ltd operates as an employment business, supplying workers to clients under contracts for services. We're subject to:

  • The Employment Agencies Act 1973
  • The Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003, which govern how we deal with clients ("hirers") and workers ("work-seekers"), including the restriction on charging work-seekers for finding them work, and the rules on transfer fees covered in our terms of business

The Employment Rights Act 2025 is changing rules for agency and zero-hours workers in stages through 2026 and 2027. We'll update these terms as each change takes effect.

Enforcement of these regulations is moving to the Fair Work Agency, a new single enforcement body. This doesn't change our obligations, only who checks them.
