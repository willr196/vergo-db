8. Taking on a worker directly (temp-to-perm)

If you want to take on a worker we've introduced or supplied to you directly (whether you employ them yourself or they're supplied to you by another agency after an introduction from us), tell us first. This section explains what happens next, in line with regulation 10 of the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003.

You will always have a choice. Instead of paying a transfer fee, you can keep hiring that worker through VERGO for a further extended-hire period of {{TRANSFER_WEEKS}} weeks, on the same rates as before, at the end of which they transfer to you free of charge. That choice is yours to make, not ours.

If you'd rather pay a fee than take the extended-hire option, the fee is {{TRANSFER_FEE}}.

This only applies if direct employment begins within the "relevant period" defined by the regulations: broadly, within 8 weeks of the worker's last assignment with you through VERGO, or 14 weeks from their first assignment with you, whichever ends later. Outside that period, no fee applies at all.

No fee is ever charged to the worker in connection with this: not for taking up work with you, and not for taking up work found through any other agency. That's a legal requirement (regulation 5 of the same regulations), not just our policy.
