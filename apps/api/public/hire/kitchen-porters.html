What the shift involves

01

Before service.

Wash-up station set, bins lined, deliveries moved in, and the kitchen ready for the chefs to start.

02

Through service.

Pots, pans and service ware turned round fast enough that the pass never waits on clean kit.

03

Clear down.

Kitchen broken down and cleaned, waste out, and the space left the way the venue or client expects.

It is physical work on long days, and it is the first thing a busy kitchen notices the absence of. VERGO books it as a proper role, not as an afterthought to the floor.

Who books kitchen porters

  • Caterers working out of a venue kitchen or a temporary one
  • Venues and hotels covering holidays, sickness or a heavy week
  • Production companies and studio kitchens feeding a crew
  • Private clients with a dinner large enough to need a kitchen behind it

Both one-off events and regular weekly cover. If the days repeat, say so, and VERGO will hold the same people on them where it can.

What to send when you enquire

  • Call time and finish time, including early starts
  • Covers, and whether it is one service or several through the day
  • The kitchen: on-site, temporary, or a production unit
  • What the wash-up is: machine, sinks, or both, and where waste goes
  • Whether the role includes prep, deliveries or anything beyond porter work
  • Whether the booking repeats, and on which days

Questions

What does a kitchen porter actually cover?

Wash-up through service, keeping pots, pans and service ware moving back to the pass, bins and waste, and the clear down at the end. Say if the role also includes basic prep or deliveries and the brief will cover it.

Can you cover early starts and long days?

Yes. Early starts and long days are normal in this work, and VERGO books for the shift you actually need rather than a standard evening. Give the real call time and finish time when you enquire.

Can we book the same porters every week?

Where the pattern is regular, VERGO sends the same people wherever it can. It is easier to hold the same crew when the days are known in advance rather than booked week to week, so say the pattern when you enquire.

Is the rate different for kitchen porters?

No. Kitchen porters are on the same rate as waiting staff, bar staff, runners and hosts, with the same four-hour minimum. Chefs and senior kitchen roles are quoted individually.

Need porters for a service or a season?

Send the dates, the kitchen and the call times, and you get confirmed names back the same day, between 8am and 10pm.
