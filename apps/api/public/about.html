Our story

About us

A London staffing agency that also puts on events. Give us your idea and we’ll staff the entire thing.

Where it started

I come from a family event-staffing business, where the job was always to find the best staff for each event, not just whoever was available.

Compare that with the staffing apps most people use now. Jobs are posted for anyone to accept, and nobody vets who turns up. I thought the work deserved better, so I started VERGO with actual care for the work that gets done.

That has grown into an agency with one direction and no other: give people and events the best possible solution for what they need.

Where we’re going

I love getting creative and making things work, and that is where VERGO is heading: a staffing agency that also puts on events.

Bring us your idea and we’ll build it and staff the whole event for you, from the themes and decor to the team on the night. Great prices, and great solutions.
