About

Staffing and events, built from the floor up.

VERGO is a London staffing and events company. It employs hospitality staff for private parties, weddings, venues, productions and branded nights, and takes on the events those rooms turn into.

Why it works this way

I grew up around event staffing. The lesson that stuck was simple: pick the person for the room, not whoever is free.

Apps post a shift and hope someone good picks it up. VERGO doesn’t. Everyone is interviewed before they join the roster, briefed before they arrive, and employed on PAYE, with payslips, holiday pay, and pension where it applies.

That is the staffing side. The events side grew out of it. Once you are sending the right people and writing the brief for the room, running the night is the next step. Christmas, Halloween, themed teams, brand nights: the same people, the same standard, and a lead on the floor.

Where it’s going

I founded VERGO in 2025. The direction is a serious staffing operation, and an events side that runs on that same operation.
