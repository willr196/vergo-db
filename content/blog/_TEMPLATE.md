## In short

- Four to five bullets. Write these last, then move them here.
- Each one answers the question outright and stands alone out of context.
- This is the block AI systems lift into answers, so it carries the post.
- At least eight words each. Concrete numbers, not adjectives.

## First question, phrased the way someone would type it

Answer it in the first sentence. Then show the working.

Second paragraph. Short declarative sentences. Say what things cost. If the
honest answer is uncomfortable, say the uncomfortable thing.

## Second question

Every post needs at least one table of real figures. If a post can't support a
table, it's the wrong post.

| What to check | What a good answer sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| Row label | The figure, and what it covers. |
| Another row | Another figure. |

Where statutory figures appear, add the caveat: these are correct for 2026/27,
and you should verify them on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/national-minimum-wage-rates)
before relying on them.
