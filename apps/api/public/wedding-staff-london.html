Planning Wedding Staffing

Wedding staffing should be planned around the order of the day. Guest arrival, ceremony timing, drinks reception, food service, speeches, evening guests, and bar close all affect the team size.

VERGO does not need the final version of everything before you make an enquiry. Send the draft timings and what is still undecided, and the staffing plan can be shaped around the current brief.

Details to send

  • Date, venue, postcode, and access details
  • Guest numbers for day and evening
  • Food and drink service format
  • Timings for arrival, meal, speeches, and finish
  • Dress code and on-site contact

Moments That Affect Staff Numbers

Wedding staffing is often shaped by transitions: guests arriving from the ceremony, drinks reception service, moving into dinner, speeches, bar opening, evening guest arrival, cake service, late food, and close-down. If those timings are unclear, staff may be planned around the wrong pressure point.

VERGO can help with individual roles or the full support team. If your caterer is covering some areas and you only need gaps filled, include that in the brief so responsibilities are clear.

FAQs

Can VERGO supply staff for a private home wedding?

Yes. Include details about access, kitchen setup, bar setup, and who is coordinating on site.

Can you provide staff just for the evening reception?

Yes. Send evening guest count, bar plan, arrival time, and expected finish.

Can staff work alongside my caterer?

Yes. Clarify which responsibilities sit with the caterer and which sit with VERGO staff.
