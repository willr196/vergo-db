How the day runs

01

Drinks reception.

Trays circulating, a bar that does not build a queue while photographs are happening, and glasses cleared as they are put down.

02

The dinner.

Guests seated, courses landing together, wine kept topped up, and the room reset around the speeches rather than through them.

03

The evening.

Room turned for dancing, evening food out, the bar running on, and the clear down once the last guest has gone.

Three different events in one day. The brief covers all three before anyone arrives, which is the difference between staff who ask what happens next and staff who already know.

Who books wedding staff

  • Couples who have hired a venue and found the staff are not part of it
  • Planners building a team around a caterer they have already chosen
  • Dry-hire venues that need a floor and bar team for the booking
  • Caterers with the kitchen covered and the front of house to fill

VERGO supplies people only. Catering, hire items, glassware and stock come from you or your suppliers, and everyone is briefed on what they will be working with.

What to send when you enquire

  • The date and the venue, and whether it is dry hire
  • Guest numbers for the day and for the evening, if they differ
  • The running order, even a rough one: reception, dinner, speeches, dancing
  • The service style, and who is doing the catering
  • The real finish time, and whether the bar runs to the end
  • Who runs the day: you, a planner, the caterer or the venue

Questions

Our venue is dry hire. Can you staff the whole day?

Yes. Dry-hire venues are a lot of what VERGO does. Waiting staff, bar staff, kitchen porters, runners and hosts are all on one rate, so you can book what the day needs rather than taking a package.

How many staff will our wedding need?

Send the guest number, the service style and the running order and VERGO will propose the staff for your day rather than applying a ratio to it. A sharing-style dinner and a plated three-course need different numbers for the same guest list.

The evening runs past midnight. How is that charged?

Hours past the confirmed finish are billed in 30-minute blocks, and work after midnight is charged at plus 25%. Weddings reach midnight more often than not, so give the real finish time and it is in the quote from the start.

Who do the staff take direction from on the day?

Whoever you name: your planner, your caterer, the venue, or you. That goes in the brief with the running order so nobody is looking for an instruction during the drinks reception.

Got a date and a venue?

Send the date, the venue and roughly how the day runs, and you get confirmed names back the same day, between 8am and 10pm.
