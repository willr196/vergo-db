What to Include in a FOH Brief

Front-of-house staffing is easier to plan when the brief explains the guest journey. A drinks reception, seated dinner, awards evening, and VIP lounge each need a different rhythm and different levels of supervision.

Send the run of show, venue plan, service style, and expected pressure points. VERGO can then recommend whether the team needs waiters, runners, hosts, supervisors, or a mix.

Useful details

  • Guest count and service format
  • Arrival, food service, speeches, and finish times
  • Dress code and expected presentation standard
  • Number of rooms, floors, or service zones
  • On-site lead and venue access instructions
