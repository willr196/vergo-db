Answers

The usual questions.

How many staff do we need for a Christmas party?

Send the guest number, the service style and the timings and VERGO will propose the staff for your event rather than applying a ratio to it. A seated dinner needs different staffing from a drinks reception with canapés.

Our party runs past midnight. How does that work?

Hours past the confirmed finish are billed in 30-minute blocks, and work after midnight is charged at +25%. Give the real finish time when you enquire and it’s in the quote rather than on the invoice.

How late can we book Christmas staff?

Ask whatever the date. Enquire between 8am and 10pm and confirmed names come back the same day, so you’ll know quickly either way. More notice means more choice.

What happens if someone doesn’t turn up?

VERGO puts a replacement on site within the hour. If it can’t, that shift isn’t charged.

Can we cancel a Christmas booking?

More than 48 hours before the booking starts, there’s no charge. Inside 48 hours it’s 10% of the confirmed booking charge, and inside 24 hours it’s 25%. The full terms are on the terms page.

December fills early.

Send the date and the guest number. VERGO comes back with names and the cost, the same day for enquiries between 8am and 10pm.
