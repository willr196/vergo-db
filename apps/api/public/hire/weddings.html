Questions

Our venue is dry hire. Can you staff the whole day?

Yes, dry-hire venues are a lot of what we do. Build the team the day needs rather than taking a package.

How many staff will our wedding need?

Send the guest number, the service style and the running order. We recommend a team for your day rather than a ratio.

Who do the staff take direction from?

Whoever you tell us: your planner, your caterer, the venue, or you. It goes in the brief with the running order.
