What they do behind the bar

01

Set up.

Bar built to your plan, stock and ice where they need to be, garnish prepped before doors.

02

Service.

Working to your list, whatever it is. Pouring wine and beer, or making drinks to spec when the list calls for it.

03

Close down.

Bar cleared, stock counted if you want it reconciled, and the space handed back.

The rush lands twice: doors, and again when a seated dinner stands up. Staffing for the peak rather than the average is what makes a bar feel calm.

Who books bar staff from us

  • Venues covering a busy week, a second room or a late licence
  • Caterers running a dry-hire event where the bar is theirs to staff
  • Brands and production companies running pop-ups, launches and activations
  • Private clients with a party at home or in a hired space
  • Agencies needing overflow cover at short notice

We supply people only. Stock, glassware, ice and bar kit stay with you or the venue.

What to tell us when you enquire

  • Guest numbers, and whether the bar is serving all of them at once
  • What is being served: wine and beer, a short cocktail list, or a full list
  • The bar layout, how many serving positions it has, and who is stocking it
  • Timings, including doors, any seated break in service, and the real finish
  • Whether it is cash, card or a hosted bar, and who handles the till

Send that and we will recommend how many people the bar needs rather than applying a ratio to your guest list.

Questions we get asked

Do you supply the drinks, glassware or bar kit?

No. We supply people only. Stock, glassware, ice and bar equipment come from you, your venue or your caterer. Tell us what the bar is set up with and the team is briefed on it before they arrive.

Can your bar staff work to our cocktail list?

Yes. Send the drinks list when you enquire and it goes into the brief, along with the bar layout and the timings. If the list is involved, say so early so we can put the right people on it.

How late can the bar run?

As late as your venue licence allows. Hours past the confirmed finish are billed in 30-minute blocks, and work after midnight is charged at plus 25%. Give us the real finish time and it is in the quote rather than a surprise on the invoice.

Can you cover a shift at short notice?

Ask whatever the date. Enquire between 8am and 10pm and you get confirmed names back the same day, so you will know quickly either way. We take a lot of overflow work from venues and other agencies.

Need a bar covered?

Send us the date, the venue and what is being served, and you get confirmed names back the same day, between 8am and 10pm.
