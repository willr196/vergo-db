Why it is not an events booking

01

The clock is the call sheet.

Breakfast before the crew turns over and lunch inside a fixed break. The schedule is set by the shoot, not the kitchen.

02

The days are long.

Early starts, and finishes that depend on how the day has run. We book to the real hours rather than an optimistic estimate.

03

It repeats.

The same crew, the same unit, day after day. People who already know the kitchen are worth more on day six than day one.

Continuity is the thing productions ask us for most. Book the dates as a block and we will hold the same people across them wherever we can.

Film studio catering

Case study

A production caterer needed a reliable kitchen team to feed cast and crew on a working film set. We supply the cooks and a kitchen porter on staggered shifts, covering prep, service and clear-down across 10-hour production days. It's an ongoing weekly contract of around 110 staff hours, with the same people each week, so the caterer never has to re-brief a new team.

What to tell us when you enquire

  • The shoot dates, and whether they run consecutively
  • Call times and expected wrap for each day
  • Crew numbers, and how many services a day that means
  • The kitchen: a unit, a studio kitchen or a location set-up
  • Which roles you need, and whether any are senior
  • Location and access, including parking and how early people can get in

Questions we get asked

Can you cover a 5am call time?

Yes. Early call times and long days are the normal shape of this work, and we book to the unit's schedule rather than a standard hospitality shift. Send the call sheet times when you enquire.

Can we have the same crew for the whole shoot?

That is what we aim for on a run of dates, and it is much easier to hold when the dates are booked in advance rather than day to day. Tell us the shoot dates up front and we will build the crew around them.

How are cooks and chefs charged?

Cooks, chefs and other senior roles are quoted individually rather than on the standard rate. Kitchen porters and catering assistants are on the same one rate as the rest of our roles, with a four-hour minimum.

Do you supply the catering itself?

No. We supply people only. We staff production caterers and studio kitchens rather than providing the food, the unit or the equipment.

What will the team wear?

Kitchen blacks and safety shoes as standard, or whatever the unit asks for. Tell us when you book.

Got a shoot in the diary?

Send us the dates, the call times and the crew numbers, and you get confirmed names back the same day, between 8am and 10pm.
