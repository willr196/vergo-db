How it works

Casting, not just staffing.

We select people around the role, the atmosphere and the level of interaction the event needs, then brief the team before they arrive.

01

Tell us the idea.

Even half an idea. The venue, the date and the feeling you are after is enough to start.

02

We propose a team.

Who does what, how far they go with guests, and what makeup or costume the concept needs.

03

They arrive briefed.

Roles are clear before doors. On larger bookings a VERGO lead coordinates it on site.
