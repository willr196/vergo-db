Planning the Staff Team

Corporate events tend to be judged on flow. Guests notice slow bars, unclear welcome points, long gaps between courses, and staff who do not understand the tone of the room. A clear brief helps avoid that.

VERGO uses details such as guest seniority, schedule, venue layout, catering format, and brand expectations to recommend the right mix of staff.

Useful brief details

  • Run of show and peak guest arrival time
  • Guest profile and dress code
  • Service points, bars, and floor layout
  • Food and drink style
  • On-site contact and venue access notes

How Corporate Staffing Differs by Event

A breakfast briefing may need hosts, registration support, coffee service, and light clear-down. A client dinner may need experienced waiting staff, runners, a bar team, and a service lead. A launch event may need more guest-flow support, drinks service, and flexible runners who can adapt as the room changes.

The best brief explains what guests should experience, not just how many staff are needed. That makes it easier to match the team to the tone of the event.

FAQs

Can staff be briefed on brand tone or guest profile?

Yes. Include the guest profile, brand expectations, dress code, and any language or etiquette notes in the brief.

Can VERGO provide bar and FOH staff together?

Yes. Corporate events often need both, especially for receptions, launches, and hospitality suites.

Can you support early starts or late finishes?

Yes, subject to availability. Share call time, finish time, and access instructions clearly.
